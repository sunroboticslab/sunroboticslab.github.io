Write one short paragraph, around 4 to 6 sentences. Describe what this facility item is, what it is used for, and what capability it adds to the lab. Keep the description understandable for students, collaborators, visitors, and people outside the specific project. Avoid writing a detailed technical manual; this page should give a clear professional overview.

## Highlights

- Write one key capability, feature, or typical use here.
- Write another important capability, feature, or research use here.
- Write one more highlight if it helps explain why this item is useful.
- Add a fourth highlight if needed.
