# 5 Segment Robot

![5 segment robot](/assets/img/facilities/5_segment_robot.jpg)

The 5 segment robot is a custom robotic platform used in the lab for studying multi-segment robotic motion, control, and experimental validation. It supports experiments involving segment-level motion, shape change, and robot behavior under different control or sensing strategies. This platform can also be used to compare physical robot behavior with models, simulations, or motion tracking results.

## Highlights

- Multi-segment robotic platform for experimental validation
- Useful for studying robot motion, control, and shape tracking
- Can support demonstrations and comparison with simulation results
